Privacy Policy

Household Finance Agent · Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. About this application

Household Finance Agent is a private, non-commercial tool operated by an individual for organising and analysing their own household finances. It is not offered to the public as a financial service.

2. Information processed

If the owner connects a bank account, the application may process account information made available through the bank and an authorised account-information provider, such as account identifiers, balances, transaction dates, amounts, currencies, descriptions and counterparty details. The owner may also add categories, notes, household allocations and reimbursement links.

3. How information is obtained

Bank information is accessed only after the relevant account holder completes the bank's authentication and consent process. The application does not ask the account holder to provide online banking passwords or one-time passcodes to the application.

4. Where information is stored

The intended application design stores its working database and exported reports on the owner's own computer. The public website containing this policy and the terms is hosted by GitHub Pages and does not contain bank credentials, account balances or transaction records. If the owner separately chooses to place files in a synchronised folder or another storage service, that service's own terms and privacy policy also apply.

5. Purposes and use

Information is used only to consolidate personal and household accounts, classify income and expenses, identify transfers and reimbursements, and produce personal reports. It is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or intentionally disclosed to unrelated third parties.

6. Service providers

When bank connectivity is used, the bank and the account-information provider process information as part of providing that service and under their own terms and privacy notices. GitHub hosts this static legal-information website and may process ordinary website access data under GitHub's policies.

7. Retention and deletion

The owner controls the local database and exported files and may delete them from the devices and locations where they are stored. Revoking bank-access consent prevents future access through that consent, but does not itself delete previously downloaded local copies or records held by the bank or account-information provider.

8. Security

The owner should protect the computer and local files with appropriate device security, keep private keys and access tokens secret, and avoid sharing exports containing personal financial information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Other account holders

An account may be connected only by its account holder, or by a person who is legally authorised to act for that holder, using the bank's own consent process. One person's consent is not treated as consent on behalf of another account holder.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the application changes. For privacy questions, contact the application owner using the data-protection contact email supplied in the Enable Banking application registration.

This policy describes the intended operation of a private personal tool. It is not legal advice and does not replace the privacy notices of banks or account-information providers.
